## Qubit design at Qilimanjaro

My bachelor's thesis at Qilimanjaro Quantum Tech focused on comparing and optimising superconducting-qubit designs. I designed readout and Hamiltonian tunability metrics alongside the theory and measurement teams, then built a modular, scalable Python optimiser with interchangeable physical models and metrics.

The search combines Latin hypercube sampling with simulated annealing from the best candidates. I ran the framework on MareNostrum 5 using **over 1,700 concurrent CPU cores**.

The **[thesis report](https://github.com/DavidDiestreR/TFG-David-Diestre-public/blob/main/report.pdf)** explains the physical reasoning behind the metrics and the design of the optimiser. The [public code](https://github.com/DavidDiestreR/TFG-David-Diestre-public) is also available.

## Other technical work

At Seeslab, I built a Python/RDKit workflow to study molecular reconstruction with the pretrained MolForge transformer. I investigated generalisation to novel molecules and sensitivity to input noise, including cases where high fingerprint similarity hid reconstruction errors.

Two course projects here:

- **[Custom AutoML regressor](https://github.com/DavidDiestreR/AutoML-AdvancedML)**: simulated annealing for joint model selection and hyperparameter tuning across four regression families, tested on three datasets alongside TPOT and auto-sklearn baselines.
- **[Optical ML for potato quality](https://github.com/DavidDiestreR/potato-dry-matter-optics-ml)**: an engineering project exploring RGB and near-infrared sensing for dry-matter estimation, with an MLP regressor and a defect-detection proof of concept.
